## Disclosure and revocation

Two owner-private tables hold immutable disclosure admissions and durable non-removable
heads. Organisation disclosure is required; an optional source scope may further restrict it.
A denial may commit with no admission, source or O head. No permit is seeded by migration.
Only postgres may write the controls; this owner-recorded basis is disclosure control,
not governance authority or semantic truth.

An admission captures the exact current D revision under a global operational statement
barrier. Re-entry requires an explicitly new valid admission for that revision; old used
or unused admissions cannot lift later denial. Validity does not rely on wall-clock
ordering. Reads and writer admission check the exact admission/revision and handling basis.
D denial survives failed/rolled-back O repair, completed repair and direct re-enable
attempts. The implementation supplies M3 controls and guards, not a reconciliation worker
or the broader future D/T/S revocation machinery.

The existing acquisition/hydration statement checks broad D once, then at most one keyed
optional source D/admission per selected hit. It never traverses policy-time agent/history
fan-out. Missing, denied or stale disclosure returns CONTENT_WITHHELD and empty hits.
The TypeScript parser accepts that one additional closed unavailable reason.

## Source binding and SAFE

An ALWAYS INSERT trigger verifies the live same-tenant agent and system before an immutable
observation/projection can persist. The O guard repeats binding on enable or target advance.
Source SHARE locks last until commit; live-source order is agent then its system after D.
BEFORE STATEMENT barriers precede D/O row locks, including empty scopes.
Only READ COMMITTED operational control/admission writes are supported.

Code/name/type and non-NULL business domain are verbatim or rejected. PROJECTED description
must be exact present SAFE text; WITHHELD is always allowed with NULL. A related system
must match tenant/ID/code/name or be genuinely absent. No trim, truncation, normalization
or sanitized substitution. Exact identity/document recipes are unchanged.

The read also validates binding/SAFE on selected historical observations, containing
pre-successor forged labels even when all identity hashes and the raw descriptor fingerprint
match. Source drift remains SOURCE_CHANGED/SOURCE_UNAVAILABLE with empty hits and no fallback.
