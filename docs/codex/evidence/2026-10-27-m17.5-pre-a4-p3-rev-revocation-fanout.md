## TRANSACTION / CAS

ADR §20.5 requires a denial-only durable D/T/S transition consulted by the successor read path to be independently committable regardless of downstream fan-out size. Once committed, it denies new reads even while dependent-head repair is incomplete. The complete dependent-head fan-out is its own atomic transaction; every update uses expected revision, exact +1 and asserted per-row/total counts. Compare no-op state before mutation. Initial insertion asserts absence, revision 1 and one inserted row. A fan-out CAS/count/membership failure rolls back that entire transaction; orchestration recomputes and retries without undoing the committed denial. Failed/over-bound repair is escalated and visible; it never restores disclosure or removes denial. No batching semantics are specified.

Lock hierarchy: broad scope barriers before children; A3 disclosure/scope head then O by PK under C text ordering; A2 repository/root lock then P then A by deterministic PK. Multi-scope parent locks are acquired as a complete ordered set. No locking SQL is added.
