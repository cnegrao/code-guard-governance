## Authored Architecture (R4B)

- Mapping ownership derives only from `gov_repo.governance_policies.organisation_id` through `policy_id`.
- A count-only preflight aborts before structural DDL or backfill when a mapping references a missing policy, a policy without tenant ownership, a missing mandate, a tenant-local mandate owned by another tenant, or a duplicate policy/mandate pair.
- `policy_mandate_mappings.organisation_id` is backfilled only from governance policies and made NOT NULL.
- `governance_policies` receives the minimal `(organisation_id, policy_id)` candidate key needed for a composite mapping FK.
- The former policy ID-only FK is replaced by `(organisation_id, policy_id)`, preserving policy-delete cascade behavior.
- `gov_repo.mandate_mapping_guards` contains one private tenant-neutral technical counter per mandate and no ownership, membership, audit, lifecycle, or business-version state.
- Mapping relationship writes and mandate ownership changes lock affected guard rows by ascending `mandate_id` with `NOWAIT`, perform a real guard counter update, and then validate the relationship.
- Mapping ownership is derived under `FOR KEY SHARE NOWAIT` protection of the governance policy; the mapping path does not explicitly lock the mandate business row.
- A deterministic `BEFORE UPDATE OF organisation_id` guard clause validates caller-explicit tenant assignment against the new protected policy before the general derivation trigger runs. Explicit conflicts raise `23514`; policy-only reassignment continues to derive the new policy tenant without caller maintenance.
- Separate PostgreSQL 17 AFTER statement triggers use transition tables for mapping INSERT, mapping UPDATE, and mandate UPDATE bulk sets.
- Global mandates remain valid through NULL `mandates.organisation_id`; tenant-local mandates must match the policy tenant.
- The broad authenticated SELECT policy is dropped, and target-table privileges are revoked from `PUBLIC`, `anon`, and `authenticated`.
- The historical service-role path is preserved and remains separate ADR-P08 work.
- No API, view, RPC, application route, or runtime consumer is added.

## Concurrency Recovery Contract (Canonical)

`55P03` (`lock_not_available`), `40001` (`serialization_failure`), and `40P01` (`deadlock_detected`) fail the complete Unit of Work. The caller must roll back the entire transaction, discard transaction-local results, and retry the Unit of Work from its beginning. Retrying only the failed statement or relying on rollback-to-savepoint plus statement retry is NOT canonical for this protocol.

Future application handling must use bounded retry with backoff and jitter. Retry exhaustion is an operational concurrency failure and must not be reclassified as a tenant or business-invariant error. No application retry implementation is part of R4B; any future savepoint-specific workflow requires separate evidence and design review.

## Indexes

- `(organisation_id, policy_id)` supports the tenant-safe FK and organisation-leading access.
- `(mandate_id, organisation_id)` supports mandate-side FK access and the mandate tenant-change guard.
- The `mandate_mapping_guards` primary key supports lifecycle lookup, deterministic ordered locking, and serialization updates without a speculative secondary index.
- The existing unique `(policy_id, mandate_id)` remains the policy/mandate pair constraint.

## Existing-Data Safety

The preflight raises deterministic integrity SQLSTATEs with counts only: `23503` for missing parents, `23502` for missing policy tenant ownership, `23514` for cross-tenant ownership, and `23505` for duplicate pairs. It does not expose row or tenant identifiers. It does not rewrite either endpoint, delete or merge a mapping, assign a tenant, make a mandate global, or otherwise repair inconsistent business data.
