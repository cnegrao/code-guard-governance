## Source evolution

Version IDs are immutable. A new NIST revision, ISO edition, DCAM model, DMBOK revision/translation or legislative amendment gets a new version record, provenance/access decision and requirement identity before remapping. Never edit an old mapping to point at a new edition. A later implementing/delegated act is a separate instrument, not part of the base Regulation pin.

Official EUR-Lex indexed metadata exposes later changes and consolidated versions; this pack intentionally imports neither their substance nor a current-law mapping. Confirm the operative version with an authorized reviewer before any future legal mapping. Changed, withdrawn or inaccessible inputs put claims on hold pending review; historical projections retain their original versions and cutoffs. No autonomous legislation decomposition or Normative Intelligence is introduced.
