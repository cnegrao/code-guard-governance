# Gov IA — Product Blueprint V4

## Product definition
Gov IA / CG-AG OS is a vendor-neutral **Agent Governance Control Plane**. It discovers, registers, qualifies, governs, traces, audits and progressively observes/contains AI agents across heterogeneous technology environments.

## Core governance chain
`Policy → Responsibility → Control → Risk → Action/Decision → Evidence → Metric → Decision/Audit`

## Primary product capabilities
- Agent Discovery
- AI/Agent Inventory & Registry
- Agent Passport 360°
- Canonical Metadata & Enterprise Enrichment
- Privacy & Sensitive Data Discovery
- Capability / Permission / Identity / Authorization Governance
- Policy & Regulatory Governance
- Controls & Assessments
- Risk & Exposure
- HITL / Approvals / Decisions
- Evidence & Governance Ledger
- Incidents & Response
- Relationships / Lineage / GraphOS
- Metrics & Governance Objectives
- Native Governance Lenses

## Lenses

- Audit
- FinOps (future)

## Commercial evolution
- Gov IA Core
- Gov IA Analytics
- Gov IA Enterprise Integrations
- Gov IA Graph Intelligence
- Gov IA Runtime / FinOps / Response

## Product boundary
Gov IA complements enterprise platforms such as Informatica IDMC, Microsoft Purview/Entra, Databricks, AWS, Google Cloud and ServiceNow. It does not attempt to replace enterprise catalogs, cloud IAM or data platforms.
