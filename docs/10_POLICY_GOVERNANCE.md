# Policy Governance

## Chain
`External Mandate → Tenant Policy → Procedure/Rule → Control → Applicability → Agent/AI System → Assessment → Evidence → Decision → Ledger`

## Rules
- Global regulatory mandates/catalogs are distinct from tenant policies.
- Each tenant owns its policy set.
- Gov IA may provide policy templates/starter packs.
- Generated or suggested policy content is draft until human adoption/approval.
- Policy versions are immutable historical references once used in governance evidence/assessment.
- Applicability can use canonical metadata, including custom classifications and privacy/security attributes.

## Example
`CUSTOM_2 = RESTRICTED` or `privacy_category = FINANCIAL_DATA` can make a policy/control applicable to an agent, which then requires assessment/evidence and influences governance decisions/metrics.
